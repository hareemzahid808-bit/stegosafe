🧠 How StegoSafe Works

  1. LSB Steganography — Each pixel's RGB channels have their least significant bit replaced with one bit of your payload. The change is imperceptible to the human eye.
  2. Payload Framing — A 14-byte header STEG magic, version, flag, 32-bit length, and CRC-32 checksum precede your data. This guarantees accurate extraction.
  3. Optional AES‑256‑GCM — When a password is supplied, your message is encrypted using AES-256-GCM with a PBKDF2-derived key (100,000 iterations, SHA-256). Random salt and IV are generated per operation.
  4. 100% Client-Side — No images, messages, or passwords are ever transmitted. Everything runs in your browser.

📦 Payload Format

[ "STEG" ][version:1][flag:1][len:4][crc32:4][data:N]

flag = 0x00 → plaintext UTF-8
flag = 0x01 → AES-256-GCM encrypted
              data = salt(16) | iv(12) | ciphertext+tag

Capacity = ⌊(width × height × 3) / 8⌋ − 14 bytes

⚠️ Limitations

  • Only lossless formats work — never re-save as JPEG.
  • Resizing, rotating, or re-editing the stego image destroys hidden data.
  • Screenshots and messenger apps may alter pixels.
  • Steganography hides presence, not content. It does not guarantee undetectability against forensic analysis.
  • Store your password safely — it cannot be recovered.

🔐 Cryptography Details

  • Cipher: AES-256-GCM (authenticated encryption)
  • KDF: PBKDF2-SHA-256 · 100,000 iterations · 16-byte salt
  • IV: 12 bytes, cryptographically random per message
  • Auth Tag: 16 bytes appended automatically by WebCrypto (tamper detection)
